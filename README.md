# 📊 Marketing Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing marketing campaign performance using **Microsoft Power BI**. The objective was to transform raw marketing data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, data analysis, interactive dashboards, and visual storytelling.

I analyzed and cleaned a dataset containing approximately **7,000 rows**, developed multiple analytical views, and created interactive bookmark-based navigation to explore different aspects of marketing performance.

The dashboard helps understand revenue generation, profitability, marketing expenditure, customer engagement, and conversion performance across different marketing channels and time periods.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall marketing campaign performance.
- Evaluate revenue and profit generation.
- Monitor marketing expenditure and profitability.
- Analyze impressions, clicks, and conversions.
- Compare the performance of different marketing channels.
- Identify monthly trends and business opportunities.
- Generate actionable insights for better marketing decisions.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard's displayed metrics and visualizations:

1. **Strong Profitability:** The dashboard reports 781.31K in total profit against 1.04M in revenue, indicating a strong overall profit contribution.

2. **Marketing Channel Performance:** Email generates the highest revenue among the four displayed channels, at approximately 302.49K.

3. **Revenue Contribution:** Social Media contributes approximately 295.32K, followed by Search Engine at 269.16K and Direct Mail at 175.5K.

4. **Monthly Revenue Trends:** March records the highest monthly revenue at approximately 118K, while December records the lowest at approximately 29K.

5. **Audience Engagement:** The campaigns generated approximately 570K impressions and 56K clicks, demonstrating substantial audience reach and engagement.

6. **Conversion Performance:** Approximately 3K conversions were generated from the marketing campaigns.

7. **Profit Margin:** The dashboard displays a profit margin of 74.95%, indicating a strong reported profitability level.

*Note: These insights reflect the values visible in the main dashboard. Additional findings can be added from the individual analytical views.*
